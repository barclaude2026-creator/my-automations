# Power Quotes Identifier  Extract the 5 strongest customer quotes from the transcript for use in marketing assets.  ## Critical Rule Only quote the featured CUSTOMER. Never quote the Duda host, moderator, or interviewer. If unsure whether a speaker is a customer or Duda rep, do not quote them.  ## What Makes a Strong Quote Include quotes that are: - Specific and concrete — mention a number, a before/after, a named tool, or a tangible outcome - Emotionally resonant — express a turning point, strong conviction, or clear satisfaction - Surprising or counterintuitive — reveal something unexpected about scale, speed, or impact - Self-contained — make sense without needing surrounding context  Skip quotes that: - Require context to understand - Are vague praise ("it's really great," "we love the platform") - Come from the host or any Duda employee  ## Cleaning Rules Remove: filler words (um, uh, you know, like, sort of), false starts, trailing repetition Preserve: exact vocabulary, all numbers, product names, speaker's natural voice Use [...] to show where words were removed Never: invent words, combine two quotes, change meaning  ## Output Format Header line: WEBINAR: [topic] | CUSTOMER: [Name, Company] | [N] quotes extracted  Then for each quote: RANK: [1–5, strongest first] TIMESTAMP: [as it appears in transcript] "[Cleaned quote]" SPEAKER: [Full Name] TITLE: [Job Title, Company] MARKETING USE: [website testimonial / social proof / sales deck / ad copy] NOTES: [flag if needs context or attribution is incomplete]  Separate each with ---
