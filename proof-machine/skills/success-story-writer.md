# Success Story Writer  Transform the webinar transcript into a polished, publication-ready Duda customer success story.  ## Step 1: Extract Before writing, identify from the transcript: - Customer name, company, role, location, industry - Challenges before Duda (scaling, quality, time, cost, technical debt) - Duda features used (APIs, white-label, AI tools, Dynamic Pages, etc.) - Results — quantitative (metrics, percentages, volume) AND qualitative - Direct quotes from the customer (3–5 candidates)  ## Step 2: Write the Story  Structure (all sections required):  ### Headline Format: [Customer Name] + [key outcome] + with Duda Lead with the transformation. Example: "Websmart migrates 3,000 websites and unlocks new growth with Duda"  ### Key Results 2–4 bullet metrics. Short and scannable. If exact numbers unavailable, use: "Significant reduction in time-to-publish without additional headcount"  ### Customer Background Who they are, what they do, what pressure led them to seek change. 2–3 short paragraphs. No Duda mention yet.  ### The Challenge What wasn't working before Duda. Specific: manual processes, scaling limits, technical limitations. 1–2 paragraphs. Use a customer quote if one fits.  ### The Solution How Duda addressed the challenge. Name specific features. Include 1–2 direct customer quotes attributed by full name and title.  ### The Results Measurable outcomes + qualitative wins. Use a quote to humanize the numbers.  ### Looking Ahead What's next for the customer. End on aspirational but grounded note.  ## Writing Rules - Third person throughout - 700–900 words - 2–3 direct customer quotes minimum, attributed: "Full Name, Title, Company" - Short paragraphs (2–4 sentences max) - No em dashes - No buzzwords: "seamless," "revolutionary," "game-changing," "robust" - Never invent metrics — qualify instead if needed - Output in clean Markdown
