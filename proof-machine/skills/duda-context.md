# Duda Context — Foundational Knowledge Layer  ## Who Duda Is Duda is a professional-grade website builder and fleet management platform built for digital agencies, SaaS platforms, and hosting companies. It is not a consumer product. It is an operational infrastructure layer for web professionals building and managing websites at scale.  Duda combines a visual drag-and-drop editor with white-label capabilities, API-first architecture, and AI-powered production tools — enabling teams to build faster, manage more clients, and deliver higher-quality sites without the chaos of traditional stacks.  Core positioning: Duda is the "production-grade alternative" to mass-market builders. While Wix and Squarespace compete for the DIY consumer, Duda has cornered the B2B2C segment — powering agencies, yellow-pages companies, hosting providers, and vertical SaaS platforms.  ## The Two ICPs  ### ICP 1: Digital Agencies High-volume web shops, SEO agencies, and marketing firms managing 50–1,000+ client sites. They need operational control, not creative chaos. - Roles: Agency Owners, Operations Directors, Head of Web Delivery - Pain: WordPress maintenance, plugin hell, client handoff friction, margin compression, scaling without headcount - They evaluate tools on: speed to publish, client control, support quality, margin per site  ### ICP 2: Vertical SaaS Platforms Software companies for specific industries (CRM for dentists, gym management software) who want to bundle a website builder to reduce churn and increase ARPU. - Roles: Product Managers, CTOs, Partnership Leads - Pain: Customers churning to all-in-one competitors, no engineering resources to build in-house, need 100% white-label - They evaluate on: API stability, white-label completeness, speed to market  ## Key Differentiators - vs. Wix Studio: Duda = operational efficiency and control. Wix = creative freedom. Duda locks down design systems so clients can't break sites. - vs. Webflow: Webflow = visual coding, steep learning curve. Duda = visual assembly, faster for non-technical teams at scale. - vs. WordPress: WordPress = plugin hell, security patches, constant maintenance. Duda = closed, managed, stable. - vs. Squarespace: Beautiful defaults, not built for scale, no true white-label. - Core moats: True white-label, fleet management at scale, AI in professional workflow, dedicated partner support.  ## What Makes Great Proof Content - Features agency or SaaS customers (not solo entrepreneurs) - Leads with operational/business outcomes, not design praise - Uses specific metrics when available (sites built, time saved, churn reduced) - Speaks to the transition from chaos to control - Uses the customer's own words - Makes the prospect think: "That company is just like us."  ## Tone Rules for All Assets - Confident and specific — never vague or corporate - Human and direct — like a smart colleague, not a press release - Outcome-first — lead with the result, then explain how - Never invent metrics — only use what the customer explicitly stated - Avoid: "seamless," "revolutionary," "game-changing," "robust," "leverage" - Agency content: narrative and relatable - SaaS content: technical and ROI-focused
